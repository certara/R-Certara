developed by Certara.

  

### Webinar Overview

The bioequivalence of biosimilars is difficult to prove using
traditional NCA approaches due to non-linear PK, or/and long half-life
as such a lot of research has been ongoing to conduct a model-based
approach.

The model-based approach operation characteristics was studied using a
simulation-reestimation approach.

In this webinar we will cover:

- How to design a simulation re-estimation study
- How to write efficient R and RsNLME workflows that build the dataset,
  fit the model, conduct the required analysis and collect and compile
  results
- How to present the results using graphics and tables
